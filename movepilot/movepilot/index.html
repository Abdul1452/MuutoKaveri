Privacy, accessibility and sources

Privacy

MovePilot has no accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking. Your answers stay in this browser tab and disappear when you close it. Only the city name is sent to our server, which uses it to fetch the weather forecast and does not store it.

Accessibility

MovePilot is built to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA: it works with a keyboard and screen readers, colours meet contrast requirements, and it follows your light or dark mode setting. Found a problem? Open an issue in the project repository.
